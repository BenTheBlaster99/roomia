Roomia — GLB regeneration checklist
====================================
Goal: replace weak catalog GLBs with models that match real product look AND metres.

Pipeline (furniture-3d-gen / your GPU machine)
----------------------------------------------
1. For each furniture_items row with a product photo (image_url):
   - Export / note: id, name, category, target W×D×H (metres)
2. Run your generator with the product reference image.
3. Verify orientation (upright, front faces +Z or your studio convention).
4. Measure the baked mesh; scale so world size ≈ target metres.
5. Upload GLB to storage (Supabase Storage or R2).
6. Update furniture_items.model_url (+ notes with dims if no dim columns yet).
7. Smoke-test in /studio: place item, check selection box vs real scale, rotate, render.

Target metres by category (defaults in lib/studio-constants.ts)
--------------------------------------------------------------
Sofa:          ~2.1 × 0.85 × 0.80
Bed:           ~1.6 × 2.00 × 1.05
Chair:         ~0.70 × 0.75 × 1.00
Coffee Table:  ~1.10 × 0.60 × 0.45
Dining Table:  ~1.40 × 0.80 × 0.75
Wardrobe:      ~1.80 × 0.60 × 2.10
TV Unit:       ~1.60 × 0.45 × 0.55
Side Table:    ~0.50 × 0.50 × 0.55
Bookshelf:     ~0.80 × 0.30 × 1.80
Rug:           ~2.00 × 1.40 × 0.02
Light:         ~0.40 × 0.40 × 0.55  (ceiling pendants, not floor lamps)
Curtains:      ~1.50 × 0.05 × 2.40

Batching
--------
Do 5–10 SKUs per batch. After each batch: open Studio, place each, photoreal one scene.
Do not rebuild all room_presets until a critical mass of hero SKUs (sofa, bed, chair, table) look right.

Runtime dims (until DB stores metres)
-------------------------------------
lib/catalog-mapper.ts maps name overrides + CATEGORY_DIMS.
Never ship undersized “testing” dims — sanitizeDimensions() enforces floors.
